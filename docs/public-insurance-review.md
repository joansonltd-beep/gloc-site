# Public insurance content review

Reviewed 17 November 2026 for the Jamaican public website. This is a reference catalogue, not a confirmation of current new-business availability or distribution authority.

## Editorial checks

Seven original guides explain life, health, critical illness, personal accident, pensions and annuities, investment-linked products and group benefits. They distinguish a health card from a policy, health reimbursement from critical illness payments, and pension accumulation from annuity income. They omit unconfirmed rates, benefit amounts and eligibility ages. Product-specific policy wording takes precedence.

Sources are linked on each guide. Cross-references include Guardian’s Jamaica catalogue, JN Life’s life and group pages, its retirement scheme page, Sagicor Jamaica individual and group catalogues, Sagicor Know Your Benefits, and the Financial Services Commission’s pension information. BCIC and ICWI were consulted for local accident-cover terminology without treating motor-policy benefits as stand-alone accident-plan terms.

## Items requiring confirmation

- Current availability, agency distribution, underwriting, benefits, exclusions, waiting periods and brochure editions must be confirmed for an individual application. No website summary is a quotation.
- Sagicor Triple Protector Plus pages contain inconsistent figures. JN AIDE’s page also contains terminal-illness/death-benefit wording that should be confirmed against its actual policy. These figures and ambiguous provisions are not reproduced in the guides.
- Direct automated requests to some Sagicor URLs returned HTTP 403. Indexed official pages corroborated catalogue and group information, but this is not a complete live link or policy-edition certification.
- JN individual product pages returned no direct brochure PDF links. Linked newsletters remain labelled newsletters. The retirement handbook and FAQ brochure were downloaded from the live JN retirement page and checked for PDF signatures.
- Service-only, historical/government references, offshore products, creditor/mortgage products and specialist schemes outside the selected scope are not advertised in the public catalogue.
